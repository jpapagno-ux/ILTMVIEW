Wild Earth Stays

The archive of remarkable places, worldwide.

A personal record of exceptional stays across every continent we work in — recorded, not listed. Every entry exists because someone from James Wild & Company has actually stayed there, returned when it mattered, and put a name to why it made the cut.

Entry No. 1.0Why Now

Advisory got noisy right when it needed to get quiet.

Search results, sponsored placements, and AI-generated "top 10" lists have replaced firsthand knowledge with volume. Every stay looks the same from far enough away.

Wild Earth Stays exists to put a human record back at the center — a small, growing archive a traveler or advisor can actually trust, built on the same standard whether it's read by a person or handed to an assistant to search.

Forty-four years of firsthand travel, finally given a structure built for how people plan now.

Entry No. 1.1Why It's Different

Not a marketplace. A record.

Most stay platforms compete on inventory size. Wild Earth Stays does the opposite — the value is in what's been left out.

Personally Vetted

Visited before recorded

Every entry is added after a firsthand stay. Jack's Camp — Entry No. 041 — has been visited six times since 1994 and is still under review.

No Marketplace Noise

No pay-to-list model

There's no booking fee that buys a listing and no algorithm deciding what surfaces first. Entries are added when they're earned.

One Advisor Relationship

A name, not a filter

Clients get a recommendation with a reason attached, from a person who can answer the follow-up question honestly.

Entry No. 1.2Hand-Selected Partners

What it takes to enter the Ledger.

We work with a small number of properties, deliberately. Most properties considered for the Ledger don't make it in — here's what we're actually looking for.

01
Character over category

A genuine point of view — not a checklist of amenities that reads the same as every other listing.

02
Firsthand only

We don't add anything we, or someone we trust completely, haven't experienced ourselves. No submissions, no press-trip fast-track.

03
Small and considered

Fewer than 100 entries total across the entire archive, added only when a place genuinely earns its place.

04
Direct relationships

We work straight with the property — no OTA-style booking layer sitting between us and where a client actually stays.
